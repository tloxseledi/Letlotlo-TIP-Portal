Temporary Import Permits for foreign-registered vehicles

Create an account, pre-register your trip, and track your permit from entry to exit.

Who is it for?

In transit

Driving through Botswana to another country. Give your exit border and destination.

Visiting

Tourism or family visit. Give where you will stay in Botswana.

Business or work

Trips for meetings, projects or deliveries. Give your host or company.

Returning resident

Residents re-entering with a foreign-registered vehicle.

How it works

1. Account

Sign up once and save your details.

2. Pre-register

Add vehicle and trip information.

3. Border check

Show your reference and documents; officials verify.

4. Exit

Record exit or request an extension.

What to bring

  • Valid passport or national ID
  • Vehicle registration papers (plus owner’s consent letter if not the owner)
  • Valid driver’s licence
  • Proof of vehicle insurance valid for Botswana

FAQ

Is the permit issued online?

You pre-register online. The permit is issued after verification at the border.

What if I stay longer than planned?

Request an extension from My permits before expiry.

Does this replace insurance?

No. You still need valid insurance for Botswana.
